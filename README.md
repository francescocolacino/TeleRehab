
# 🏥 TeleRehab
### Smart Rehabilitation. Real-Time Monitoring. Personalized Therapy.

TeleRehab is a desktop application designed to support clinicians in creating, managing, and running personalized rehabilitation therapy protocols, while allowing patients to follow their prescribed exercises through an interactive and monitored environment.

The application features a deterministic simulation engine that generates realistic patient telemetry, enabling real-time monitoring of vital signs and perceived physical conditions throughout therapy sessions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🩺 **Personalized Therapy Protocols**  
  Clinicians can design and configure customized rehabilitation protocols tailored to individual patient needs.

- 📊 **Real-Time Patient Monitoring**  
  Continuous monitoring of simulated patient telemetry, including:
  - ❤️ Heart Rate (BPM)
  - 🫁 Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂ %)
  - 😓 Perceived Fatigue Score
  - 🩹 Perceived Pain Score

- ⚙️ **Deterministic Simulation Engine**  
  A deterministic engine simulates patient physiological data and subjective feedback during rehabilitation sessions.

- 🚨 **Safety Controls**  
  Real-time monitoring and safety mechanisms help clinicians supervise therapy sessions and identify potentially unsafe conditions.

- 👨‍⚕️ **Clinician & Patient Workflow**  
  Two complementary roles: clinicians manage therapy protocols, while patients follow their assigned rehabilitation programs.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

TeleRehab aims to provide a controlled digital environment for rehabilitation management and monitoring.

Its main objectives are to:

- Simplify the creation and management of personalized therapy protocols.
- Provide clinicians with continuous visibility into simulated patient conditions.
- Support patient-centered rehabilitation workflows.
- Demonstrate the integration of telemetry simulation and safety monitoring in a desktop application.
